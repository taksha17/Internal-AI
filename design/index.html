internal-ai PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Private by design.

Only company data, approved by a data owner. Gates are decided by a person, never by an agent. Every action is audited.

  • Random initialisation, approved internal data, no pretrained weights.
  • Human gates; the requester can never approve their own gate.
  • Append-only audit log, chained by hash.
