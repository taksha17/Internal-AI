Train a classifier

A task classifier is a small head over an encoder you already trained. It learns the answers your data carries when the source had an answer column, and falls back to the built-in routing rules otherwise. It is not promotable: promotion asks for an evaluation on a frozen retrieval set, and a classifier's held-out split is not one.
