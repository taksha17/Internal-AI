The problem

Own the model. Keep the data. Prove the result.

For a regulated team, the hard question is the third one. Banks, hospitals and defence cannot send their data out, and cannot ship a model they cannot explain.

01

Renting has a ceiling.

Every prompt leaves your boundary, and the weights, the model card and the upgrade schedule belong to someone else. For a regulated team, that makes the best model the least usable.

02

Open weights change the maths, not the work.

Downloading a model takes minutes. Turning your workflows into evals, your experts' knowledge into training data and your production failures into fixes takes a platform.

03

Ownership is a loop.

Workflow becomes eval, expertise becomes data, failure becomes improvement, and all of it carries forward to the next base model. Most teams build this by hand, once, and let it rot.

04

Governed, or it does not ship.

Who approved this data? Who can see what the model learned? Can you prove it after an audit? If the answer is not built in, it will not pass review.

Platform

Everything between your data and a model you own.

One control plane, one data plane, and the training, evaluation and serving that surround them. Agents do the engineering work; people take every irreversible decision.

Control plane

A dependency-ordered task graph, ten role agents with declared capabilities, gates that need a named second person, budgets at five scopes, a kill switch, and an append-only hash-chained audit log.

Data plane

Ingestion, exact and near-duplicate removal, PII and secret scrubbing, quality and language filters, immutable dataset versions, a tokenizer trained from scratch, and leakage-safe splits.

Training

A from-scratch transformer that records its commit, config, dataset hash and seed. It checkpoints, resumes, caps cost, streams metrics, and stops itself on divergence or a loss spike.

Evaluation

Frozen eval sets that only a person can approve, an independent evaluation agent, promotion gates, and model cards. A score is three numbers on the same frozen set, or it is not a score.

Serving

Authenticated inference and permission-aware retrieval, per-user rate limits, and rollback. Every answer carries the passages it was shown, so an answer can be checked against its own evidence.

Adaptation

For an install that decides an existing open base is acceptable: a rank-8 adapter over one pinned base, streamed from disk, recorded on every model card by repo, revision and licence.

How it works

Three steps to a model you own.

Point the platform at your data. The rest is governed automation.

01

Ingest and prepare

Connect your sources — documents, tickets, resolved questions. The data plane handles exact and near-duplicate removal, PII and secret scrubbing, quality and language filters, and immutable dataset versioning. A tokenizer trained from scratch on your corpus.

  • Sources stay inside the boundary
  • Redaction is measurable (20,455 in stress run)
  • Leakage-safe splits by construction
02

Train and evaluate

Launch a from-scratch transformer or an alpha adapter over a pinned base. The trainer records commit, config, dataset hash, and seed. It checkpoints, resumes, caps cost, streams metrics, and stops itself on divergence or a loss spike.

  • Provenance mode: scratch_only or gated alpha
  • Frozen eval sets, human-approved only
  • Promotion gate: adapter vs base vs keyword
03

Serve and improve

Authenticated inference with permission-aware retrieval. Every answer carries the passages it was shown. Traces become triage cases — failure feeds the next training run. Gates, audit, and offline default govern every step.

  • Per-user rate limits and rollback
  • Trace-to-triage pipeline built in
  • Kill switch engages on one click

Governance

The rules are the product.

These hold through every redesign, and the repository refuses to weaken one to make a result look better. A suppressed check is a lie with a green tick.

  • No training on third-party model output. Distillation is a non-goal; task data comes from internal documents and resolved questions.
  • Raw records never leave the boundary. Schemas, aggregate statistics, redacted samples, code and logs may.
  • Frozen eval sets are write-protected. A change is a new version behind a new gate.
  • Gates are human-only. And the requester cannot decide their own.
  • The audit log is append-only and hash-chained. Enforced by triggers; every write goes through AuditLog.record.
  • Every spend path goes through the budget manager. At every scope, with no path that bypasses it.
  • The kill switch engages on one click. Releasing it needs a different person.
  • Provenance mode is explicit. scratch_only refuses every base load; open_weight_allowed is gated.
